OA Consulting›Industries

Five industries. One commercial discipline.

OA Consulting's practice spans five primary sectors — each with its own regulatory environment, performance challenges, and commercial logic. The consulting approach is adapted for each. The standard for results is the same.

Industry 01

Government & Public Sector

Government ministries, departments, parastatals, and state revenue agencies represent our largest and most impactful client segment. The scale of value at stake in Nigerian government procurement, IGR collection, and public service delivery creates correspondingly large advisory opportunities. We have designed revenue recovery programmes for state revenue agencies, procurement reform initiatives for federal parastatals, and capacity development programmes for civil service institutions.

The combination of OA Group's professional services capability (OA & Co) with OA Consulting's advisory and OA Group's contracting arm (Pearl Primus) creates a uniquely integrated offering for government clients requiring multi-disciplinary support.

IGR OptimisationProcurement ReformRevenue RecoveryCapacity DevelopmentProcess RedesignBPP Compliance
Industry 02

Private Sector

Nigerian medium and large enterprises face a distinctive combination of challenges — currency volatility, infrastructure constraints, talent competition, and regulatory complexity — that requires advisory grounded in local operating conditions. We work with privately-held Nigerian businesses and multinationals with Nigerian operations on strategic repositioning, performance improvement, M&A preparation, and operational cost reduction.

Our M&A advisory capability positions us for premium mandates from private equity-backed businesses and owner-managed enterprises approaching a liquidity event or considering a strategic acquisition.

Business AdvisoryM&A AdvisoryPerformance ImprovementStrategic PlanningCost OptimisationOrganisational Design
Industry 03

NGO & Development Finance

International NGOs, development finance institutions, and donor-funded project management units require advisory that combines programme delivery rigour with commercial discipline. Donor accountability requirements create specific governance, procurement, and financial management obligations that most commercial consulting firms do not adequately understand. OA Consulting's experience with donor environments enables us to support NGO clients with the combination of technical capability and institutional awareness that these engagements demand.

Programme Management SupportProcurement AdvisoryGovernance FrameworksCapacity DevelopmentFinancial Management Training
Industry 04

Banking & Financial Services

Banks, microfinance institutions, and financial services firms face a specific performance challenge set: NPL recovery, operational efficiency under CBN cost-to-income pressure, and the strategic decisions created by consolidation pressure and digital disruption. OA Consulting's debt recovery advisory and M&A capabilities are particularly relevant for financial institution clients navigating these pressures.

Debt Recovery AdvisoryOperational EfficiencyM&A AdvisoryProcess OptimisationStrategy
Industry 05

Manufacturing

Nigerian manufacturers face a cost structure defined by energy price exposure, logistics cost, currency-denominated input costs, and the infrastructure constraints that characterise production in Nigeria. Process optimisation — not strategic repositioning — is the primary lever available to most manufacturing clients in the current environment. OA Consulting's process and cost optimisation capability delivers measurable unit cost reduction across production, procurement, and logistics functions.

Process OptimisationCost ReductionSupply Chain ReviewProcurement ManagementOperational Design
