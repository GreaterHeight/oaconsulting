Privacy Policy

OA Consulting Limited · Effective Date: 1 January 2025

Last updated: January 2025 · NDPR Compliant

OA Consulting Limited ("the Company," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of individuals who interact with our website and consulting services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019, the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, and applicable international data protection standards.

1. Who We Are

OA Consulting Limited is a management consulting firm registered in Nigeria (RC: [CAC: RC-XXXXXX]), with its registered office at 4, Aderoju Adewuyi Street, Off Alfred Olaiya Street, Opebi-Ikeja, Lagos State, Nigeria. We are a member of OA Group Nigeria.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Contact form submissions: name, email address, telephone number, organisation name, role, area of interest, and message content
  • Training registration enquiries: contact and organisational details
  • Client engagement: commercial data, organisational information, and other information necessary to deliver consulting services

Information collected automatically

  • Browser type, device information, and operating system
  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Pages visited, session duration, and referring URLs
  • Cookie data (see Cookie Policy below)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to consulting enquiries and deliver professional services
  • To communicate about training programmes and related services where you have expressed interest
  • To improve our website and content
  • To comply with legal and contractual obligations
  • To prevent fraud and protect system security

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Consent: Newsletter subscriptions and non-essential communications
  • Contract: Delivery of professional services under formal engagement
  • Legitimate interests: Business operations and website improvement
  • Legal obligation: Compliance with professional and regulatory requirements

5. Data Retention

  • Client engagement records: minimum 7 years in accordance with professional standards
  • Contact enquiries: 12 months unless an engagement commences
  • Website analytics: aggregated and anonymised after 24 months

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We may share data with service providers under strict data processing agreements, regulatory bodies where legally required, and other members of OA Group Nigeria where you have specifically requested group services.

7. Your Rights

Under the NDPR and NDPA, you have the right to access, correct, erase, and object to processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact: info@oaconsulting.oagroupng.com

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies (required for website function) and analytics cookies (placed with consent only). Manage preferences through the consent banner on your first visit.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. Our website is served over HTTPS and access to client information is restricted to authorised personnel.
