Our Approach

Five stages. One commercial outcome.

OA Consulting's engagement methodology is built around a single discipline: understanding the problem before proposing the solution. Every engagement follows a structured five-stage process that prevents the most common consulting failure — recommending before diagnosing.

Start with a Diagnostic

Problem Definition

Before any engagement begins, we invest in understanding the real problem — not the presenting symptom. Initial diagnostic sessions establish the commercial question the engagement must answer.

Diagnosis

Structured assessment of the client's current state — financial data, process mapping, stakeholder interviews, and benchmarking against comparable organisations and industry standards.

Strategy

Development of the intervention strategy — specific recommendations, sequenced actions, resource requirements, and measurable outcomes tied to the client's commercial objectives.

Implementation

Where OA Consulting distinguishes itself from advisory-only firms — we remain engaged through implementation, providing hands-on support, accountability structures, and adaptive management as conditions change.

Review & Handover

Formal post-engagement review measuring outcomes against the defined commercial question. Sustainable handover to the client's internal team with documentation, training, and knowledge transfer.
