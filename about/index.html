Consulting grounded in commercial reality

OA Consulting Limited is the management advisory arm of OA Group Nigeria — structured around a single conviction: that consulting which does not move commercial outcomes is not consulting. It is decoration.

Who We Are

The problem-solving arm of OA Group

OA Consulting Limited was established as a specialist management advisory practice — distinct from the group's professional assurance function at Olusola Adekanola & Co — to serve organisations that need intervention, not just certification.

Our practice is anchored in three commercial realities that define the Nigerian advisory market. First, that revenue leakage is pervasive and recoverable — and that recovery mandates are among the most commercially valuable engagements a consulting firm can hold. Second, that advisory without implementation is incomplete — organisations do not need another report to file. Third, that Nigerian market complexity demands practitioners with genuine local experience, not imported frameworks applied without adaptation.

As a member of OA Group, OA Consulting has privileged access to the group's professional services, financial services, real estate, and contracting capabilities — enabling integrated mandates that advisory-only firms cannot assemble from a single platform.

Our Philosophy

How we think about consulting

Diagnosis before prescription

The most common consulting failure in Nigeria is recommending before understanding. Every OA Consulting engagement begins with a structured diagnostic phase — because the presenting problem and the actual problem are rarely the same.

Outcomes, not outputs

Reports, frameworks, and presentations are outputs. Revenue recovered, costs reduced, and processes improved are outcomes. Our engagements are scoped around outcomes and measured against them — not against the volume of documentation produced.

Nigerian market specificity

Global consulting frameworks applied without adaptation to Nigerian operating conditions produce recommendations that look credible and fail in practice. Our interventions are designed for the regulatory, infrastructure, and commercial environment that our clients actually navigate.

Implementation accountability

Advisory without implementation support is incomplete. OA Consulting remains engaged through implementation — providing adaptive management, accountability structures, and the honest feedback loop that internal teams cannot always provide.

Group Position

The advisory engine of OA Group

Within the OA Group ecosystem, OA Consulting plays a specific role: translating audit findings (from OA & Co) into improvement programmes, connecting advisory mandates to financing solutions (through MOL), and routing project implementation to commercial execution (through Pearl Primus).

This ecosystem position gives our clients access to integrated solutions that no single-discipline advisory firm can assemble — across professional services, finance, real estate, and commercial contracting within a single trusted group.
